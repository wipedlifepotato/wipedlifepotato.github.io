- **Day 1** — not noticeable.
- **Day 2** — cognitive performance decreases, the body looks for stimulants and gets tired.
- **Day 3** — cognitive functions deteriorate significantly; you start struggling even with things you know well. Even in chemistry that you have seen and reviewed many times and that seemed clear, you just stop being able to think straight.
- **Closer to day 4** — aggression and hallucinations begin.
